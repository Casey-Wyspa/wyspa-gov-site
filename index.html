Lead concept

Autonomous foiling powerboat

A small uncrewed surface vessel that lifts out of the water on foils. Less wetted area means less drag at speed, longer range per kilowatt-hour, and a steadier sensor platform in a seaway.

Most small USVs are planing hulls that slam and burn energy in a chop. Foil-borne flight decouples the hull from the waves. That benefit depends on getting foil sizing, cavitation margins and flight control right, and that work is what we do.

We propose a phased program: a performance and trade study, then a subscale demonstrator, then a full-scale prototype built with a partner yard.
