Lead concept

Hydrofoil USV

A 20 ft class unmanned hydrofoil craft with a proa hull: one main hull and one outrigger. It's designed to fly above the chop for a steadier, more efficient ride. Target missions: port and harbor security, maritime domain awareness, logistics and ocean observing.
